Frequently Asked Questions

Project & Services

Common questions about how Smart Choice Contractors USA handles construction and remodeling projects.

What services does Smart Choice Contractors USA provide?

We provide residential and commercial construction services, remodeling, kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, roofing, electrical, plumbing, drywall, painting, repairs, additions, drawings, permit support, and project planning throughout Tampa Bay and surrounding Florida areas.

Do you work on both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. We work with homeowners, investors, property managers, commercial tenants, business owners, and property owners who need remodeling, repairs, buildouts, maintenance, or construction planning.

Do you provide free consultations?

We offer consultation options depending on the project type. Some projects can begin with a phone review, while others require an in-person visit, measurements, photos, drawings, or a paid consultation depending on complexity.

How long does a remodeling project usually take?

Project timelines depend on scope, permits, inspections, material availability, change orders, design decisions, hidden conditions, and local building department review. Small repairs may take days, while kitchens, bathrooms, additions, and full renovations may take weeks or months.

Can you help with project planning before construction starts?

Yes. We can help with project planning, site review, measurements, scope organization, material lists, construction drawings, permit preparation, and estimate development before work begins.

Can you create 3D renderings or walkthroughs?

Yes. For certain projects, we can help create 3D visuals, renderings, layout concepts, and project images to help owners understand the design before final decisions are made.

Do you provide material takeoffs and quantity estimates?

Yes. We can prepare material takeoffs and quantity estimates for flooring, drywall, paint, framing, roofing, tile, cabinets, trim, and other construction materials when the project scope and measurements are available.

Can you help with insurance-related repair projects?

We can assist with repair scopes, photos, estimates, and project documentation. Insurance claim approval, coverage, and payment decisions are handled by the insurance carrier and policy terms.

Do you provide emergency repair services?

Emergency availability depends on schedule, trade type, weather, location, and safety conditions. Electrical, plumbing, roofing, water damage, and storm-related repairs should be reviewed quickly to reduce additional damage.

How do I start a project with your company?

Start by contacting us through the website, booking an appointment, calling our office, or submitting a project form. Include the property address, photos, project description, budget range, and any drawings or permits you already have.

Permits, Codes & Florida Requirements

Helpful questions about permits, inspections, Florida Building Code, and county requirements.

Do construction projects in Florida require permits?

Many projects require permits, especially electrical, plumbing, roofing, structural, mechanical, additions, window and door replacements, and major remodeling. The final requirement depends on the local building department and exact project scope.

What Florida Building Code edition applies now?

Florida currently uses the 8th Edition 2023 Florida Building Code for many current permit applications. Local jurisdictions may also have procedures, floodplain requirements, forms, and review requirements.

What is a Notice of Commencement?

A Notice of Commencement is a Florida construction document related to lien law and permitted improvements. It identifies the property, owner, contractor, lender if applicable, and project start information. Some projects must record it before work or inspections.

When is a Notice of Commencement required?

Florida requirements depend on the project and statute. Florida law includes exceptions, including HVAC repair or replacement under $15,000 in certain direct contracts. Always confirm with the local building department or clerk before starting work.

Who records the Notice of Commencement?

The property owner or authorized party typically records the NOC with the county clerk when required. Some jurisdictions also require the recorded copy to be uploaded to the permit portal before inspections.

Do you handle inspections?

When permit coordination is included in the project scope, we can help coordinate inspections. Inspection requirements depend on the permit, trade, project phase, and local building department.

Do I need drawings for a remodeling permit?

Many remodeling permits require drawings, especially when walls, structural framing, electrical layouts, plumbing layouts, mechanical systems, exterior changes, or life-safety conditions are involved.

Do flood zones affect remodeling work?

Yes. Flood zones may trigger additional requirements, including substantial improvement or substantial damage review, FEMA-related documentation, elevation requirements, and special city or county review.

What happens if hidden code issues are found during construction?

Hidden issues such as unsafe wiring, damaged framing, plumbing defects, rot, termite damage, unpermitted work, or non-code conditions may require additional work, change orders, inspections, or engineering review.

Can I buy my own materials for a permitted project?

Sometimes, but owner-supplied materials must meet project requirements, product approvals, code requirements, sizing, warranty requirements, and schedule needs. Incorrect materials can delay the project or increase labor cost.

Licensing, Insurance & Legal Documents

Answers about contractor licensing, contracts, lien documents, warranties, and payment paperwork.

What is the difference between a certified and registered contractor in Florida?

A certified contractor license allows work statewide under the licensed scope. A registered contractor license is typically limited to local jurisdictions where the contractor holds competency. Always verify the license type and scope before hiring.

How can I verify a Florida contractor license?

You can verify many contractor licenses through the Florida DBPR license search website. You can search by name, license number, city, county, or business information.

Do written contracts matter for construction projects?

Yes. Written contracts help define scope of work, payment schedules, exclusions, warranties, change orders, responsibilities, and legal notices. A signed estimate or proposal may also become part of the project agreement.

What is a change order?

A change order documents work that changes the original scope, price, materials, schedule, or project conditions. Change orders help avoid confusion and protect both the owner and contractor.

What is a lien release?

A lien release is a document used to confirm that a contractor, subcontractor, or supplier has received payment or is releasing lien rights for a specific payment or project stage.

What is a Notice to Owner?

A Notice to Owner is a Florida lien-law notice commonly used by subcontractors or suppliers who do not have a direct contract with the owner. It helps protect lien rights and informs the owner that labor or materials are being provided.

Do you provide warranties?

Warranty terms depend on the written agreement, manufacturer warranties, material warranties, trade scope, and project conditions. Warranty coverage should always be reviewed in writing before work begins.

Can project payments be made by credit card?

Payment methods depend on the project and agreement. Some projects may allow credit card payments, ACH, checks, online payment links, or other approved methods. Fees or authorization forms may apply.

Do subcontractors need insurance?

Subcontractors should provide proper license information, insurance certificates, workers’ compensation documentation or exemptions where applicable, W-9 forms, and required agreements before performing work.

What documents should I keep for my construction project?

Keep contracts, estimates, invoices, payment receipts, permits, inspection records, change orders, lien releases, product warranties, photos, drawings, and final closeout documents.

Electrical, Plumbing, Roofing & Specialty Work

Trade-specific questions for common Florida construction and remodeling projects.

Do electrical panel upgrades require permits?

Yes, electrical panel upgrades usually require permits, inspection, utility coordination, code-compliant materials, proper grounding, and licensed electrical work.

Do new electrical circuits require permits?

Many new circuits, service changes, panel work, EV charger installations, major lighting changes, and remodeling electrical work require permits and inspections.

Do water heater replacements require permits?

Water heater replacements often require plumbing permits, safety pan requirements, expansion tank review, pressure relief discharge compliance, electrical or gas review, and inspections depending on jurisdiction.

Do roofing repairs or replacements require permits?

Roof replacements usually require permits. Some repairs may also require permits depending on size, type, location, structural conditions, and local jurisdiction rules.

Can you install Tesla or EV chargers?

Yes. EV charger installation requires review of the electrical panel, available capacity, wire route, breaker sizing, distance, wall type, grounding, permitting, and inspection requirements.

Do kitchen remodels require electrical and plumbing inspections?

Many kitchen remodels require electrical and plumbing inspections when circuits, outlets, lighting, appliance feeds, drains, water lines, islands, or layout changes are involved.

Do bathroom remodels require permits?

Bathroom remodels may require permits when plumbing, electrical, structural, ventilation, shower pan, waterproofing, or layout changes are involved. Simple cosmetic work may be treated differently by jurisdiction.

Can you work with customer-provided fixtures?

Customer-provided fixtures may be used when they are code-compliant, compatible, complete, undamaged, and approved for the intended installation. Missing parts or incompatible products may create delays or extra labor.

Do you handle drywall, painting, and finish work after trade work?

Yes, when included in the scope. Drywall repair, texture matching, painting, trim, flooring transitions, and finish work should be clearly included in the estimate or change order.

Can you help with energy-efficient upgrades?

Yes. We can help with LED lighting, smart controls, insulation coordination, efficient HVAC planning, EV charger installations, roofing improvements, window and door upgrades, and other energy-related improvements.

Financing, Scheduling & Customer Support

Questions about estimates, financing, project communication, appointments, and what happens after you contact us.

Do you offer financing directly?

We provide financing resource links and may help customers understand general options. Financing approval, interest rates, credit requirements, repayment terms, and funding decisions are handled by third-party lenders.

How fast will someone contact me after I submit a form?

Our goal is to contact customers within 8 to 24 business hours. Response time may vary based on work volume, emergency jobs, weekends, holidays, or missing project information.

What should I include when requesting an estimate?

Include your name, phone number, email, property address, project description, photos, measurements, preferred timeline, budget range, drawings if available, and any permit or insurance information.

Can I upload drawings or documents?

Yes. If your project has drawings, permits, engineering, inspection comments, insurance documents, photos, or product selections, uploading them helps us review the project more accurately.

Do estimates include materials and labor?

That depends on the estimate. Some estimates include labor only, while others include labor, standard materials, allowances, permits, or specific products. Always review inclusions and exclusions before approval.

Why can final pricing change after a site visit?

Pricing may change if the site conditions are different from the information provided, if hidden damage is found, if code corrections are needed, or if the customer changes materials, layout, or scope.

Do you provide project updates?

Yes. Depending on the project type, updates may be provided by phone, text, email, CRM portal, photos, project meetings, or jobsite communication.

Can I access my project documents online?

For some projects, customers may access documents, estimates, invoices, appointments, and project information through our CRM client portal.

Do you work in all Tampa Bay counties?

We serve Tampa Bay and surrounding areas, including Spring Hill, Pasco County, Hernando County, Hillsborough County, Pinellas County, and nearby Florida communities depending on project scope and scheduling.

What is the best way to schedule an appointment?

The best way is to use the online appointment page, submit the contact form, or call our office. Provide as much project information as possible so the correct department can respond.
