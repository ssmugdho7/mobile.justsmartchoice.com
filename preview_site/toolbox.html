Contractor Rules at Customer Homes

Field rules for contractors, installers, helpers, subcontractors, and project managers working inside occupied homes or businesses.

  • Arrive on time or notify the customer and project manager before delays occur.
  • Wear clean and professional clothing suitable for the project and customer environment.
  • Protect floors, walls, cabinets, fixtures, countertops, furniture, and landscaping before work begins.
  • Keep tools organized and maintain clear walking paths for customer safety.
  • Do not enter private rooms, closets, garages, or storage areas unless the scope requires it and the customer approves access.
  • Do not smoke, vape, drink alcohol, or use illegal substances on customer property.
  • Do not use customer bathrooms, refrigerators, tools, ladders, materials, or equipment without permission.
  • Do not play loud music, use offensive language, or argue on the jobsite.
  • Keep the work area clean at the end of each workday.
  • Take before, during, and after photos when required for documentation.
  • Report hidden damage, unsafe wiring, plumbing leaks, rot, mold, termites, or structural concerns immediately.
  • Never perform extra work without written approval, change order, or project manager confirmation.
  • Follow Florida Building Code, NEC, plumbing code, manufacturer instructions, and permit conditions.
  • Use proper PPE including eye protection, gloves, hearing protection, respirators, fall protection, and hard hats when required.
  • Respect customer privacy, security cameras, pets, children, neighbors, and HOA rules.
  • Secure tools, materials, ladders, cords, chemicals, and blades before leaving the jobsite.
  • Do not discuss pricing disputes, payments, or contract issues directly unless authorized.
  • Contact the project manager if a customer requests additional work or changes.
  • Document completed work, inspections, material receipts, and jobsite issues daily.
  • Leave the property safer, cleaner, and better organized than when work started.

Homeowner Project Rules & Expectations

Helpful guidance for homeowners and property owners to keep projects moving smoothly.

  • Provide accurate property information, permit history, HOA rules, and project documents.
  • Make product selections early to avoid delays.
  • Keep children and pets away from work areas.
  • Remove personal belongings from work areas before construction starts.
  • Review estimates, contracts, allowances, exclusions, and change orders carefully.
  • Do not request field workers to perform extra work without company approval.
  • Expect dust, noise, deliveries, inspections, and temporary disruptions during remodeling.
  • Respond quickly to approval requests, color selections, fixture selections, and layout decisions.
  • Understand that hidden damage or code corrections can affect price and schedule.
  • Keep payment schedules current to avoid project interruptions.
