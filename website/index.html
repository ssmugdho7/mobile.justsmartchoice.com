⌂ Smart Choice Mobile
Android · Testing build 0.5.1

Your Smart Choice CRM, on your phone.

Access the same account, projects and documents you already use in Smart Choice CRM. The app connects to crm.justsmartchoice.com; your records stay in the existing CRM.

Download Android testing APK Open staff CRM in browser Customer portal

Android 10 or newer on a 64-bit ARM phone/emulator. This is a debug-signed testing build, not a Play Store release. Use your existing CRM login. Actions in this app affect your live CRM records.

Get started

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone or drag it onto an Android emulator.
  2. Install and open Smart Choice Mobile.
  3. Sign in with your existing staff or customer CRM account.

Camera and microphone access is requested only when you use meeting features. For app updates, download and install the newer build from this page.
